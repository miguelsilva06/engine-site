Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Português

Engine is a training, readiness and nutrition app for endurance athletes who also train strength. This policy explains what data the app and this website use, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept and the rights you have over it. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is José Miguel Silva, Rua do Espinheiro, n.º 645, 4415-812 Sandim, Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal, tax number (NIF) 275158306. Contact for any privacy question or request: getengine.app@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer; requests go to that address.

2. Data we use

Needed to use the app (without it we cannot run your account or calculate anything):

  • Account: email address and password, or the identifier and email that Apple or Google give us if you sign in with them.
  • Basic profile: the name you choose, age (you must be 18 or over), sex, height, weight, everyday activity level, the sports you practise, your goal and weekly availability.

Optional (the app works without each of these; you give them only if you want the feature):

  • Thresholds and tests: critical power, critical speed, CSS, heart-rate thresholds, lab tests.
  • Training: sessions you log in the app (including strength sets and loads, exercises you create, routes and GPX files) and sessions imported from services you connect, with their heart-rate, power, speed and altitude series.
  • Health and recovery: heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, body weight and workouts read from Apple Health or Health Connect, only with the permissions you grant on your device, or received from a service you connect.
  • Nutrition: meals and foods you log, and your nutrition goal.
  • Daily check-in and session feedback: how you rate sleep, stress, fatigue, mood, soreness, effort and how a session felt; gut tolerance notes.
  • Notes for the coach: injuries and anything else you choose to write down for it.
  • AI coach: your questions and its answers, images and PDFs you attach, and voice notes you record (see section 5).
  • Community: a public @handle, photo, bio, the sessions, photos, captions and stats you choose to publish, your comments, the users you block and the reports you send.
  • Subscription: your plan, its status and renewal date. We never see your card or bank details: payment is handled by Apple or Google.
  • Technical: crash and error reports, a push-notification token if you allow notifications, and a record of the consents you give or withdraw (which one, when, and the version of the text you saw).

Health, training and nutrition data are health data, a special category under the GDPR. We process them only with your explicit consent, asked in the app before you enter the first number.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Running your account and subscription (sign-in, storing and showing your data, support, your plan) — performance of our contract with you, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Calculating training load, thresholds, zones, readiness and nutrition targets from your health, training and nutrition data — your explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a) and 6(1)(a). You withdraw it by deleting your account.
  • The AI coach, automatic workout summaries, the weekly review and attachments — your separate consent, Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a). Off by default; on and off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Voice notes to the coach — the same AI consent, and only when you use the microphone.
  • Connected services (importing your data, and sending planned workouts to intervals.icu) — your consent, given when you connect each service and withdrawn by disconnecting it.
  • Publishing in the community — your consent, given when you make your profile or a session public and withdrawn by making it private again.
  • Push notifications and emails about the launch — your consent (the device permission; the box on the website form).
  • Error reports, abuse prevention, usage limits and security — our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and safe, Art. 6(1)(f). You can object (section 9).
  • Reviewing reports of abusive content — our legitimate interest and legal obligations as a host of user content.
  • Invoicing and accounting records — legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c).

Withdrawing a consent does not affect what was done before you withdrew it.

4. Profiling and automated decisions

Engine automatically calculates scores and estimates about you — readiness, training load, zones, thresholds, calorie and nutrient targets — and the coach writes suggestions from them. These are information for you. The coach proposes and you decide: a change to your plan is applied only when you accept it. Nothing in Engine takes a decision about you that has legal effects or similarly significant effects without a person involved.

5. The AI coach

The coach is powered by Claude, a model made by Anthropic. It is off until you turn it on. With your AI consent:

  • When you ask the coach something, the relevant parts of your profile, recent training, health and recovery data, nutrition, planned sessions, notes and the conversation are sent to Anthropic to generate the answer. If you attach images or PDFs (Pro plan), they are sent too. To answer some questions the coach can search the web through Anthropic; the search terms are written by the model.
  • Automatic workout summary: when a new workout arrives, a one-sentence summary is generated from that workout's figures, your effort rating and what was planned for that day.
  • Weekly review: once a week, a review of the week that ended is generated from the same kinds of data.
  • Voice notes: when you use the microphone, the audio is sent to OpenAI to be turned into text. Engine does not keep the audio.

Anthropic and OpenAI process this data as our processors, in the United States, under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Under their commercial terms they do not use it to train their models. If you turn the AI switch off, nothing more is sent to either of them, the three features above stop, and the rest of the app keeps working.

6. Connected services

You decide which services to connect, and you can disconnect them at any time in Settings → Connections or in that service's own settings. We only receive what you authorise.

  • Apple Health / Health Connect: HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, weight and workouts, read on your device with the permissions you grant.
  • intervals.icu: a source and a destination. We import your activities and wellness data (HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, weight) from it, and, if you use the feature, we send your planned workouts to your intervals.icu calendar so they reach your watch. Strength sessions are never sent. Devices such as Garmin reach Engine only through intervals.icu.
  • Polar and, once available, Wahoo and Suunto (when connected): activities and recovery data, according to the scopes you approve.
  • Strava: Engine does not connect to Strava. Data previously imported from Strava has been deleted.

Each of these services is an independent controller of the data it holds; its own privacy policy applies to it.

Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or by an adequacy decision. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account exists. When you delete your account it is deleted from our live systems immediately, and Engine's access to the services you connected is revoked.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.
  • Server and technical logs: up to 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: if an account is not used for 24 months, we warn you by email or push notification; if it is still not used 30 days after the warning, it is deleted.
  • Coach conversations: until you delete them or your account.
  • Launch email list (website form): 12 months after you sign up, or until you ask to be removed; removed when you delete your account.
  • Reports of abusive content: 12 months after the report is made.
  • Record of your consents: while your account exists; deleted with it.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple or Google under their own policies; our accounting records are kept for the period required by tax law.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data and receive a copy. Settings → Export my data gives you a JSON file with what we hold about you. The 10-second series of each session (heart rate, power, speed, altitude) are not in that file because of their size; ask us by email and we will send them.
  • Correct data that is wrong: most of it you can edit in the app.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Delete account, or ask us to do it.
  • Restrict the processing of your data, in the cases the law provides for.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (the export file).
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest (error reports, security).
  • Withdraw a consent at any time: the AI switch in Settings → Privacy, disconnecting a service, making your profile private, turning notifications off, or deleting your account.
  • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you (see section 4).

To exercise any right, write to getengine.app@gmail.com. We answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority; in Portugal that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), www.cnpd.pt.

10. Community and security

Nothing is public by default. If you make your profile public or publish a session, other Engine users can see your name, @handle and photo, and the sessions, photos, captions and stats you chose to publish, and your comments. You can unpublish at any time. You can report a session, comment or profile, and block another user, from the menu on that content.

Everything you have not published can be read only by your account: each record is protected by row-level security in the database. Tokens for connected services are stored server-side and never sent back to the app. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS).

4. Definição de perfis e decisões automatizadas

O Engine calcula automaticamente pontuações e estimativas sobre ti — prontidão, carga de treino, zonas, limiares, objetivos de calorias e nutrientes — e o coach escreve sugestões a partir delas. São informação para ti. O coach propõe e tu decides: uma alteração ao teu plano só é aplicada quando a aceitas. Nada no Engine toma, sem intervenção humana, uma decisão sobre ti que produza efeitos jurídicos ou que te afete de forma igualmente significativa.

5. O coach de IA

O coach usa o Claude, um modelo da Anthropic. Fica desligado até o ligares. Com o teu consentimento à IA:

  • Quando perguntas alguma coisa ao coach, a parte relevante do teu perfil, treino recente, dados de saúde e recuperação, nutrição, sessões planeadas, notas e a conversa é enviada à Anthropic para gerar a resposta. Se anexares imagens ou PDF (plano Pro), também são enviados. Para responder a algumas perguntas o coach pode pesquisar na web através da Anthropic; os termos de pesquisa são escritos pelo modelo.
  • Resumo automático do treino: quando chega um treino novo, é gerada uma frase de resumo a partir dos números desse treino, da tua avaliação do esforço e do que estava planeado para esse dia.
  • Revisão semanal: uma vez por semana é gerada uma revisão da semana que terminou, a partir do mesmo tipo de dados.
  • Notas de voz: quando usas o microfone, o áudio é enviado à OpenAI para ser convertido em texto. O Engine não guarda o áudio.

A Anthropic e a OpenAI tratam estes dados como nossos subcontratantes, nos Estados Unidos, ao abrigo das cláusulas contratuais-tipo da Comissão Europeia. Nos termos comerciais de ambas, não os usam para treinar os seus modelos. Se desligares o interruptor da IA, nada mais é enviado a nenhuma delas, as três funcionalidades acima param e o resto da app continua a funcionar.
