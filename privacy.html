Engine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Engine is a training, readiness and nutrition app for endurance athletes who also train strength. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who processes it on our behalf, and the rights you have over it. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email address and sign-in method (email, Apple or Google).
  • Profile you enter: age, sex, height, weight, thresholds (critical power, critical speed, CSS, heart-rate thresholds, lab tests), goals, nutrition targets, injuries and notes you choose to give the coach.
  • Training: sessions you log in the app (including strength sets and loads) and sessions imported from services you connect.
  • Health and recovery: heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and workouts read from Apple Health or Health Connect, only with the permissions you grant on your device.
  • Nutrition: meals and foods you log.
  • Daily check-in: how you rate sleep, stress, fatigue, mood and soreness.
  • Coach conversations: the questions you ask the AI coach and its answers.
  • Technical: crash and error reports, and a push-notification token if you allow notifications.

2. Connected services

You decide which services to connect, and you can disconnect them at any time in Settings → Connections or in that service's own settings. We only receive what you authorise.

  • Apple Health / Health Connect: HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and workouts.
  • Strava: your activities, for display to you only. Strava data is never sent to the AI coach and is never shown to other users, as required by Strava's API terms.
  • intervals.icu, Garmin, Polar, Oura, WHOOP and other wearables (where available): activities and wellness data, according to the scopes you approve.

3. How we use it

  • To calculate your training load, thresholds, zones, readiness and daily nutrition targets.
  • To show your data back to you across the app.
  • To let the AI coach answer your questions and adjust your plan (see section 4).
  • To publish a session or profile to the community, only when you choose to.
  • To keep the app working and fix errors.

The legal bases are your consent (health data, connected services, the AI coach) and the performance of our contract with you (running your account and subscription).

4. The AI coach

The coach is powered by Claude, a model made by Anthropic. When you use the coach, the relevant parts of your profile, recent training (excluding Strava data), physiology, nutrition, planned sessions and notes are sent to Anthropic to generate the answer. Anthropic processes this data as our service provider and does not use it to train its models. You can stop using the coach at any time; the rest of the app keeps working without it.

5. Who processes your data

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and server functions. Data stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Anthropic — AI coach (United States).
  • RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play — subscriptions and purchases.
  • Sentry — crash and error reports.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.

Where data leaves the EU, it is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

6. Community

Nothing is public by default. If you set up a public profile or publish a session, other users can see what you chose to share (for example the photo, caption and metrics you selected). You can unpublish at any time.

7. Security and retention

Each record is protected by row-level security so only your account can read it. Tokens for connected services are stored server-side. We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our systems.

8. Your rights

  • Access, correct or export your data.
  • Delete your account and data in the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent for any connected service or the AI coach at any time.
  • Complain to your data-protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

Engine is not intended for children under 16.
