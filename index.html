    What Engine doesn't know.

    Most apps hide their uncertainty. Engine prints it.

    • The AI can be wrong.So it shows the numbers behind each answer, and nothing changes without your approval.
    • The AI is your choice.The coach is off until you turn it on, and one switch turns it off again.

    Questions.

    I already use Garmin or TrainingPeaks.

    Keep them. Engine reads your data and adds what they leave out: the gym, the food, and the source of each number.

    Do I need a power meter?

    No. Running uses critical speed, swimming uses CSS, and heart rate covers the rest.

    Does it work with my watch?

    Apple Health, Health Connect, Polar and intervals.icu (Garmin, COROS and Zwift through it) today. Wahoo and Suunto are in review.

    Is the AI making things up?

    It can be wrong, so it shows the numbers behind each answer and changes nothing without your approval. It is built on Claude, and it only receives your data if you turn it on.

    Is my data safe?

    Hosted in the EU (Ireland). Profiles are private by default. You can export or delete everything in the app, and the AI has an off switch.

    What happens after the trial?

    You move to Free. Your data stays visible and yours.
